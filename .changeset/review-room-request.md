Review Room: agents can ask for a review of their own PR with `request_review` (with a briefing of what, why, impact, reading order, open questions and the commands they ran). The review is listed under Requested in Screen PRs without interrupting you, the briefing shows labelled as the author's and is checked by the overview, threads can Ask author, and submitting hands the review back to the requesting session (posting to GitHub is optional). Reviews of the same stack are numbered rounds that carry their threads over.
