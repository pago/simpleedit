Remote access can now publish the phone companion through OpenTunnel, an experimental public HTTPS tunnel that works without Tailscale. Settings → Remote access offers Off, Tailscale Serve or OpenTunnel; OpenTunnel asks for a one-time acknowledgement that the address is public and protected only by the access key. Its address stays the same across restarts and is deleted when you switch away.
