The phone's PRs tab opens the Review Room too. The board lists reviews an agent requested and keeps a stack together. A stack reads as one page: the whole-stack threads, then each layer's overview or author's briefing, its threads and its diff, with line threads under their lines. You can start threads from a tapped line, ask the companion (it runs on the Mac, and the phone tells you when it needs you there), draft and edit comments, mark threads done, open the outline, and submit one review per layer with its own verdict, or send it back to the requesting session. The phone's old PR detail and Discuss with Agent sheet are replaced by the room.
