Screen PRs opens a PR or stack in the new Review Room. Findings from screening, the overview and deep review, other reviewers' GitHub comments and your own notes all become threads under their lines. Each thread has a private conversation with a read-only review companion agent (Claude, Codex or OpenCode, docked as a terminal at the bottom) and an optional draft. The overview gains a reading order that becomes the room's tour, and a repo's `REVIEW.md` steers the overview, deep review and companion. You submit one GitHub review per layer, each with its own verdict, and your drafts are posted as a pending review that a failed or interrupted post resumes. Each layer shows the new commits since you last looked. The desktop's Discuss with Agent and review composer are replaced by the room.
